The method

Not a black box.

How do you know Outcrop isn’t an AI making things up about you? The AI doesn’t decide. Here is what does.

1

The problem

A guess with a number on it is still a guess.

Many tools let an AI guess your skills and hand you a score. No one can show you where it came from.

Outcrop doesn’t guess. It doesn’t score you.

2

The rule

No sentence, no reading.

Every capability is tied to your exact words. Open it, and there they are.

  • Outcrop’s questions never count. Only your words do.
  • No support in your words, no reading.
  • Nothing is saved until you save it.
3

The framework

Twelve capabilities, written down.

  • Seven Practices: what your work changed.
  • Five Dimensions: how you did it.

In Outcrop you see them in plain language, such as “Keeping important work running under pressure.” The names live on the Capabilities page, for anyone who wants to explore them.

Each one is defined in full, in public. Read the twelve definitions on alayacrafts.com.

4

The discriminators

Every capability has a line it must cross.

Who wrote these rules? People did. Not the AI.

We built them by observing how capability actually shows up in work, and refined them as we test them.

“Strategic” and “a natural leader” are opinions. No one can prove or disprove them. So Outcrop replaced opinions with written rules: what counts, and what doesn’t.

  • Plans don’t count. Only what happened.
  • Praise and labels don’t count.
  • Someone else’s work isn’t yours.
  • One thing after another isn’t cause.

The rules are a method, not the final word. They are meant to be refined as we learn. When a rule changes, the change is logged.

5

The boundary

The AI finds. The rules decide.

The AI

Finds your exact words and the facts in them. Names no capability. Rates nothing.

The rules

Check those facts against each discriminator. Rule met: a capability appears. Not met: nothing does.

Your résumé, title, or chosen starting point only help the AI ask better questions. They never count as support, and they never decide a reading.

6

Accuracy

Curious in conversation. Strict in the record.

No single accuracy score. One number would hide where mistakes happen.

Generous in the conversation

Outcrop asks what a résumé never does, to draw out what you’d leave out.

Strict in the record

A reading needs your words to meet a rule. Nothing you didn’t say gets added.

The human checkpoint

You vouch for what happened. The rules decide what it supports. Neither stands in for the other.

7

Emerging and Established

Even strength follows a rule.

Emerging
Your record currently shows this, but not yet across distinct work contexts and over time.
Established
Supported by work stories that meet the requirement for both breadth and time.

Missing dates are never guessed. Both describe the support in your record, never you.

8

The limits

Outcrop doesn’t claim the truth about you.

It claims what can be verified: these words support this capability, under this rule.

  • It can’t verify your story. You are its author.
  • It won’t make anything up. If nothing was found, it shows nothing and gives you the chance to expand.
  • No score. No ranking. No promise of a job.
